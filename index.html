Instructions
  • Headphones: Stereo headphones must be used. Listening through external speakers will not produce Binaural Beats effects.
  • Sound frequency: Adjust left/right headphone frequencies manually or choose a preset based on your objective:
    • Delta: Relaxation, deep sleep, stress reduction.
    • Theta: Meditation, boosting creativity.
    • Alpha: Mental relaxation, reducing anxiety, promoting calm.
    • Beta: Enhancing alertness, improving focus and concentration while studying or working.
    • Gamma: Improving learning efficiency, stimulating higher cognitive functions.
  • Pink noise: Helps mask external background noise.
  • Loudness: It is recommended to keep volume below -12 dBFS (typically -18 dBFS to -24 dBFS). If listening alongside other audio, reduce it further by -10 dB to -15 dB.
